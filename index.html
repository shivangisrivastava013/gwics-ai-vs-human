Digital Literacy Keynote

5 Ways to Spot AI in the Wild

Take home these essential forensic techniques developed by researchers and digital literacy advocates to analyze synthetic media.

01

Glyph & Typography Collapse

Diffusion models understand semantic concepts better than symbolic orthography. Zoom into background posters, book spines, and street signs—AI renders melted, garbled pseudo-letters.

02

Biometric & Earring Asymmetry

Examine earlobes, fingernail beds, teeth alignment, and jewelry. AI frequently generates mismatched earrings or fused digits where hands interact with objects.

03

Polished but Hollow Prose

LLMs predict average, high-probability tokens. They lean heavily on stock metaphors (“beacon of hope”, “testament to”) and lack grounded, idiosyncratic specific nouns.

04

Acoustic Cadence & Breath Absence

Authentic human speech contains micro-hesitations, glottal friction, and inhalation pauses. Cloned voices often produce unnatural metronomic cadence without room resonance.

05

Physical Optics & Shadow Invariance

Look for physical contradictions: do shadow angles match light sources? Do reflections on wet pavement mirror true geometry? Real physics rarely lie.
