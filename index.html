Rules & Scoring

How The Showdown Works

  • One Captain Submits: One phone per table submits the team's answer. Everyone else discusses at the table and watches the projector.
  • XP Economy: Every team starts with 100 XP. Correct answers award +100 XP. Incorrect answers earn 0 XP.
  • Paid Hints: You can unlock 1 hint per question for 25 XP. The hint is private to your team.
  • Locked Submissions: Once your captain locks in your choice, your answer is sealed until the host reveals the verdict.
