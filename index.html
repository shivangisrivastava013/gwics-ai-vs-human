Rules & Scoring

How The Showdown Works

  • Individual Play: Every attendee plays on their own phone with their own name and submits their own choices.
  • XP Economy: You begin with 100 XP. Each correct answer awards +100 XP. Incorrect answers earn 0 XP.
  • Paid Hints: Need a forensic clue? Unlock 1 hint per question for 25 XP. The clue is private to your phone.
  • Locked Submissions: Once you tap your choice, your answer is sealed until the host reveals the verdict.
  • Standings: See how your human instincts rank on the live leaderboard!
